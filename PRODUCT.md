# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PTIT Learning serves students who discover, buy, and study online courses, plus teachers and administrators who manage course content, reviews, orders, promotions, and reports. Learners often use the app to resume a course, track progress, manage purchases, save interesting courses, and download certificates.

## Product Purpose

The product is a web learning platform with commerce, course catalog discovery, video lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, certificates, and role-based dashboards. Success means users can quickly find the right course, purchase with confidence, continue learning without friction, and trust operational dashboards to manage the platform.

## Brand Personality

Modern, capable, and practical. The interface should feel current and polished, but it should primarily support clear workflows and study momentum rather than behave like a decorative marketing site.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-style gradients as the dominant identity, over-decorated cards, inconsistent button and badge styles, icon/emoji mixtures that make product controls feel informal, and page-level redesigns that break existing routes or user muscle memory.

## Design Principles

1. Keep learning momentum visible through progress, next actions, and clear completion states.
2. Make commerce decisions feel trustworthy with consistent pricing, discounts, reviews, and checkout affordances.
3. Use one product design vocabulary across storefront, learner account, learning player, and dashboards.
4. Favor task clarity over decorative novelty, especially in dashboards and learning flows.
5. Modernize incrementally so existing content, routes, and role workflows remain stable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is not the primary priority for the current pass, but the UI should avoid obvious regressions: readable text contrast, visible focus states for key controls, keyboard-reachable navigation, and reduced reliance on motion or color alone for state.
